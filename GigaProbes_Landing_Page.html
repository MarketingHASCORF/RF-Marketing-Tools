Applications

Built for high-speed measurement work

Signal integrity validation — verify PCB differential impedance and S-parameter performance
BGA probing — reach fine-pitch geometries with fixed-pitch precision tips
VNA differential testing — precise differential measurements up to 70 GHz
De-embedding & fixture removal — isolate the DUT from board and probe effects
TDR impedance measurement — verify impedance along a transmission line
Failure analysis — repeatable on-board measurements for root-cause work
Also Available

VNA calibration & verification test boards

Signal Microwave and GigaProbes® high-frequency test boards give your VNA a known, golden-unit response over frequency — so you can verify calibrations, catch measurement drift, and train engineers on advanced calibration techniques.

40

40 & 70 GHz Verification Boards

50 Ohm through lines, a 25 Ohm Beatty section for TDR verification, and 100 Ohm differential lines — including connector-to-probe lines for evaluating each probe. Built on 8-mil Rogers RO4003 with ENIG finish and Signal Microwave edge-launch connectors, with magnetic feet for secure bench positioning.

LRL

LRL & LRM Calibration Boards

A hardware alternative to software de-embedding: a 40 GHz LRM board with probe test pads sets the reference plane at the probe tips, and a 70 GHz LRL board with 3″ traces supports training on advanced VNA calibration techniques.
